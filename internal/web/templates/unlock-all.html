⚠ This can damage the repository permanently

"Remove all locks" deletes every lock of {{.Path}}, also the locks of backups, forgets, prunes or checks that are running right now. A process that loses its lock keeps working: a client can then write into the repository while another process deletes or rewrites data.

  • The result can be lost or corrupted snapshots and data that cannot be recovered. restic cannot undo it, and this repository is itself the backup: there is no second copy to restore from.
  • The damage is often not visible at once. It may show only at the next check, or when a restore fails.

Locks now: {{with .Locks}}{{if .LockError}}unreadable{{else if .Locks}}{{.Locks}}, the oldest {{.OldestLock}} old{{if .StaleLock}} (older than 24 h: probably stale){{else}} — a recent lock usually means something is still running{{end}}{{else}}none (nothing to remove){{end}}{{end}}.

Use this only when you are sure nothing is running, for example when a client was shut down in the middle of a backup less than 30 minutes ago (restic treats such a lock as stale only after 30 minutes without refresh, unless it was created on this machine). For older locks, "Unlock" on the Run now tab is enough and safe.
